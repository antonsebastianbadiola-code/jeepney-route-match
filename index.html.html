How the prototype works
1. Fixed informationThe permanent side marking is displayed separately because it may not represent the vehicle's active trip at a particular moment.
2. Active informationThe current front destination represents the trip the passenger is about to board.
3. Passenger intentThe passenger selects the destination they actually need to reach.
4. VerificationThe system compares the active destination with the passenger's intended destination and flags a mismatch or signage conflict.
